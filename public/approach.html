Overview

The name is the method.

In isiXhosa, ukuva means to listen. That is where every engagement starts: listening properly, understanding completely.

Listening without doing, though, is just noise. The full offer is the second half of the word in practice: going out into the world and doing the work. You bring the vision and the resources. I bring the seniority, the network, and the hustle to make it real, then step away when it’s done.

Listen properly. Then go and do the work, with real accountability for whether it ships.

Engagement principles

The rules that keep this honest.

01 · Focus

Maximum three clients at a time

Quality of execution matters more than volume. Ukuva never runs more than three active engagements simultaneously, so every client gets full focus and genuine delivery accountability.

02 · Clarity

Not a cofounder. Not a consultant.

No partnership, no equity, no shared control. You have a vision; I execute on your behalf. When the mission is done, the engagement ends, and no dependency is created.

03 · Ownership

The Blueprint is yours to keep

The Operational Readiness Blueprint belongs entirely to you. It’s non-refundable because the research and expertise behind it are real. Execute it independently, or bring me back to deliver it.

04 · Transparency

Travel on engagement terms

Base of operations is Amsterdam; most work is remote. Where on-site presence is required, travel and accommodation are invoiced separately and transparently. Nothing hidden.

The Blueprint

The plan exists to be executed.

The output of Phase 2 is a single, practical document: the Operational Readiness Blueprint. Not a strategy deck, but an execution plan, built to be acted on in Phase 3.

  • Built for doing, not filing. Every line is something to act on, sequenced and costed.
  • Yours, outright. Run it yourself or hand it back to me to deliver. Most clients want it done.
  • Honest about cost and risk. No optimism inflation. Real numbers, real failure modes.
