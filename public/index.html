The problem

You are not short of ideas. You are short of time to bring them to life.

You have the ability, the network and the resources. What you do not have is a spare three months to write the plan, set up systems, find suppliers, hire the team and get a new thing off the ground on top of running the business you already have.

So the idea sits there. It has been sitting there for a while.

Why the usual options fall short

× [ consultants ]

Hand you a document

They describe it, then move on. You wanted it done, not described.

× [ agencies ]

Need constant direction

You do not have the hours to manage them on top of everything else.

× [ co-founders ]

Want equity and a say

In a business you intend to own outright. A stake you did not want to give.

× [ freelancers ]

Lack the seniority

Not senior enough to make the calls you would make yourself.

What you actually need is one senior person who can understand the whole picture, work out exactly what it takes, and then go and make it happen without being managed.

The Ukuva Plan

What it takes to make your idea real, costed and ready to build.

The Ukuva Plan is the core of what I do. It is a fixed-fee planning engagement that turns your idea into a clear, costed, executable plan: what needs to happen, in what order, who does it, what it costs, and where the risks are. It is written to be handed to a team and built, not to sit on a shelf.

By the end you have a plan you could execute yourself, and a standing offer to have me assemble and run the people who execute it for you. You own it either way.

  • The shape of the venture and what “done” looks like.

What I do

To be clear: I plan and orchestrate. I do not build in-house.

This is the one thing worth being precise about, because it is easy to assume otherwise.

I do

Work out exactly what your venture needs, cost it, sequence it, then assemble and coordinate the team and suppliers who make it real, using my network rather than building everything myself. I present you with options and you sign off on the direction and the risk. I am the guide who frames the decisions and runs the delivery.

I do not

Write the code, design the product myself, or run your existing business day to day. I am not your developer and I am not your COO. I am the person who gets the whole thing built and keeps it moving.

Why me

Twenty years getting hard things done, across industries.

I do not lead with adjectives. I have spent about twenty years building, launching and fixing things in payments, fintech, streaming and cross-border markets, always with real delivery accountability, never as an adviser on the sidelines. A few examples of the kind of hard things I have got done:

01

Opened PayPal to a market that was shut out

I solved the exchange-control problem that stopped South Africans from using PayPal. I built the case, secured South African Reserve Bank approval, and ran the joint build with PayPal end to end. That solution still runs today, and was later extended into Kenya.

02

Launched streaming across a continent

As a founding team member at Showmax, part of Naspers, I built the payments and subscription function from scratch and set up operations across sub-Saharan Africa.

03

Turned an acquisition into a working business

After DT One acquired MODE, I stepped in as COO and then Interim CEO to integrate the two businesses, rebuild the technical team and restructure partner deals.

That depth is why clients trust me to plan a venture properly and to know who to call to build it. The sectors change. The discipline of shipping hard things does not.
