The Ukuva Plan

The Ukuva Plan is a fixed-fee planning engagement from Ukuva Consulting. It turns your idea for a new venture into a clear, costed, executable plan: what needs to happen, in what order, who does it, what it costs, and where the risks sit. The plan is priced as a fixed fee, quoted to your requirements, and it is yours to keep.

What you get

A single, plain document you could hand to a team and build from tomorrow. It covers the shape of the venture, the sequence of work, the suppliers and people needed, a realistic cost, and the risks and decisions you will face, with my recommendation on each.

What it costs

A fixed fee, quoted firmly before I start based on the scope we agree, payable half up front and half on completion. No hourly creep. You know the number in advance.

What happens next

You keep the plan and execute it yourself, or you engage me to assemble and run the team and suppliers who deliver it. There is no obligation to do the second thing. The plan stands on its own.

How it starts

With a free 30-minute call, then a short scoping document so we both know we are building the same thing, then the fixed quote. Four steps, no surprises.
